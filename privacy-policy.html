Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Virtue HSE is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how we use it when you visit our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Company (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Telephone number (if provided)
  • Information contained within your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry.
  • Provide the complimentary guide if requested.
  • Discuss consultancy services where requested.
  • Maintain records of our communications.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information submitted through our contact form is processed securely by our website form provider (FormBold) for the sole purpose of delivering your enquiry to us.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain business records or comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection legislation, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information where appropriate.
  • Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
